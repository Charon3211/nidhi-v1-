Your questions, answered

Do I need gym experience?

No. I’ll meet you at your current level and coach each movement step by step.

Do I need a gym?

Not always. We’ll choose the equipment and space that match your plan and your current setup.

Can I train if I'm a beginner?

Yes. I’ll adjust the pace, exercise choices and progressions to your current ability.

Is nutrition included?

Nutrition support will be explained clearly before you start. I only offer guidance within my qualifications and do not promise medical nutrition advice.
