Privacy policy

NIDHI FITS respects your privacy. This policy explains what may be shared when you use this website or contact Nidhi about coaching.

Information you choose to share

If you contact Nidhi through WhatsApp, the information you send—such as your name, phone number, goals and messages—is used to respond to your enquiry and discuss coaching. If the lead form is connected to a form service, the name, contact details and goal submitted there are sent to that service for follow-up.

Analytics

Google Analytics 4 and Meta Pixel are configured to remain inactive until their account IDs are added. If enabled, they may collect standard website interaction data, including page visits and WhatsApp CTA clicks, according to their own policies.

Sharing and retention

NIDHI FITS does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by WhatsApp or an enabled form and analytics provider. Messages are retained only as long as reasonably needed to answer enquiries, provide coaching or meet legal obligations.

Your choices

You can choose not to submit information, ask what information is held about your enquiry, or request deletion by messaging Nidhi on WhatsApp at +880 1621-172535.

Important: Do not send medical records, payment details or other sensitive information through this website or WhatsApp.
